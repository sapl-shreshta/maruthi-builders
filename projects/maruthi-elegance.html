Overview

The concept behind Maruthi Elegance is "efficient premium living." The 2BHK format depends on intelligent room sizing, strong storage possibilities and common infrastructure that supports a smooth resident experience.

Premium 2BHK flats must avoid the feeling of compromise. The planning challenge was to protect living-room comfort, bedroom usability and service efficiency within a tighter footprint.
