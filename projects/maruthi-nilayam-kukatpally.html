A sold-out Kukatpally residential project on a 1000 sq. yard plot with 25 premium flats.

Overview

The concept behind Maruthi Nilayam was "organized premium living." The building was planned to accommodate a meaningful number of homes while maintaining "clarity in access, services and common spaces."

Construction Approach

The project addressed multiple demands simultaneously, including "careful coordination between structural bays, vertical movement, service shafts and parking expectations," with construction emphasizing "a sturdy RCC framework, durable masonry, reliable service systems and common area finishes that can handle regular residential traffic."

Outcome

Maruthi Nilayam's sold-out status places it firmly within our completed construction credentials, demonstrating the ability to deliver a larger apartment complex without losing focus on usability.
