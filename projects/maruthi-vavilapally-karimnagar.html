"The Home of Everlasting Relationships" — HMDA-approved luxury 3BHK apartments beside SVJC College in Vavilapally, Karimnagar, with just two spacious, no-common-wall flats on each floor.

Each floor holds only two 3BHK homes of 1625–1650 sq.ft, both east-facing, planned strictly as per Vastu with good cross-ventilation, dedicated car parking and CCTV coverage across all common areas.

Specifications

Structure
RCC-framed structure with clay-molded first-class brick masonry in cement mortar.
Joinery
Teak wood frame and shutter with melamine polish on the main door; laminated flush internal doors; UPVC sliding windows with mosquito mesh and painted MS grills.
Flooring
Granite in corridors, double-charged vitrified tiles in the living room, 800x800 vitrified tiles in dining and bedrooms, anti-skid ceramic tiles with 7 ft dado in toilets.
Kitchen
Granite platform with stainless steel sink, municipal and bore water provision, water purifier and chimney/exhaust fan provision.
Electrical
Concealed copper wiring (Havells or equivalent), geyser points in all bathrooms, TV points in the master bedroom and living room, split A/C provision in every bedroom.
Lift & Power
4-passenger automated lift (Schindler or equivalent) with granite/tile finish; 100% DG power back-up for common areas.
Security
CCTV coverage across all common areas; internet and cable TV provision in the living room and master bedroom.
Painting
Two coats of luxury emulsion over putty finish internally; primer plus two coats of exterior emulsion (Asian Paints or equivalent).

Flats are handed over for woodwork/interiors only after 100% payment. GST and registration charges apply as per Government of India / Telangana State norms.
