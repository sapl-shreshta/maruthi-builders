Our Story

Built by engineers who still visit every site.

Maruthi Builders started as a small residential contracting outfit in Kukatpally and grew into a name families across Hyderabad trust with their biggest investment — a home.

Since 2005

From one apartment block to a name families recommend.

Maruthi Builders was founded with a straightforward goal — deliver residential construction that homeowners could trust without needing to double-check every beam and bill. What began with a single apartment project in Kukatpally has grown into a portfolio of completed and ongoing developments across Hyderabad.

We are not a marketing-led developer. Every project is run by an in-house team of civil engineers and site supervisors who are present through the full construction cycle — from foundation to final handover — because that consistency is what actually protects build quality.

Milestones

Two decades, one project at a time.

2005

Founded in Kukatpally

Maruthi Builders is established, taking on its first residential apartment contracts in Kukatpally and Miyapur.

2011

Maruthi Enclave Delivered

A 20-flat complex at Paparayudu Nagar, Kukatpally is completed and fully sold — an early proof point for build quality.

2016

Maruthi Nilayam Completed

A larger 25-flat development on a 1000 sq. yard plot in Kukatpally, engineered with expanded structural specifications.

2021

In-House Engineering Team Formalized

Civil engineers and site supervisors brought fully on-payroll to guarantee consistent, accountable site management.

2026

Maruthi Meadows Underway

A 60-flat gated community in Kompally enters construction, our most ambitious project to date.
