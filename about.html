Our Story

Built by engineers who still visit every site.

Maruthi Builders started as a small residential contracting outfit in Kukatpally and grew into a name families across Hyderabad, Karimnagar and Khammam trust with their biggest investment — a home.

Since 2005

From one apartment block to a name families recommend.

Maruthi Builders was founded with a straightforward goal — deliver residential construction that homeowners could trust without needing to double-check every beam and bill. What began with a single apartment project in Kukatpally has grown into a portfolio of completed and ongoing developments across Hyderabad, Karimnagar and Khammam.

We are not a marketing-led developer. Every project is run by an in-house team of civil engineers and site supervisors who are present through the full construction cycle — from foundation to final handover — because that consistency is what actually protects build quality.

20+ Years of Building with Purpose

With more than two decades of experience in residential construction, our Founder & Managing Director has built a career around one core belief: a home is more than a structure—it is a space built for life.

From the earliest stages of planning to the final handover, his hands-on approach and deep understanding of construction have shaped the way we work. His experience spans project planning, execution, quality management, and on-site delivery, with an unwavering focus on getting the details right.

Over the years, this experience has translated into a clear commitment to quality craftsmanship, thoughtful execution, transparency, and dependable delivery.

Today, he continues to lead the company with the same values that shaped its foundation—bringing together experienced teams, trusted partners, and proven construction practices to create homes that are designed to endure.
