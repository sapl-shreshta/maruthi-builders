What We Do

Construction services built around one accountable team.

From single apartment blocks to full gated townships, Maruthi Builders handles approvals, engineering, execution and handover under one roof.

Apartment Complexes

Multi-storey residential blocks planned for efficient unit layouts, strong RCC structures and healthy natural light and ventilation. We manage everything from soil testing to final finishing across 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK formats.

  • • Structural design & RCC execution
  • • Vastu-conscious unit planning
  • • Common area & parking layouts

Independent Villas

Custom-built villas on individual plots, designed around your family's layout preferences with premium structural specifications and finishing materials of your choice.

  • • Custom floor plans & elevations
  • • Premium material selection
  • • Landscaping & boundary work

Gated Communities

Planned townships combining multiple residential blocks or villa plots with shared amenities — clubhouses, landscaping, security infrastructure and managed common areas.

  • • Master planning & zoning
  • • Amenity & clubhouse construction
  • • Ongoing community maintenance setup

Project Management

A single point of accountability across approvals, vendors, budgets and site supervision — so you're never coordinating between disconnected contractors.

  • • RERA & municipal approvals
  • • Vendor & material sourcing
  • • Milestone-based progress tracking

Structural & Civil Engineering

In-house structural engineers design and validate every foundation, column and slab specification against soil and load conditions specific to the site — not generic templates.

Vastu-Conscious Planning

Layouts reviewed for vastu compliance from the earliest design stage, balancing traditional principles with modern space efficiency and natural light.

Our Process

From site visit to key handover.

01

Consultation

02

Design & Approvals

03

Construction
