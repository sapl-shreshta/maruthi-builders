FAQ

Common questions before you reach out.

Does Maruthi Builders take on construction for privately owned plots?+

Yes. Alongside our own residential developments, we take on small residential-block construction on client-owned plots, managed end-to-end by our in-house team.

Are your ongoing projects RERA registered?+

All active Maruthi Builders developments are registered under RERA before bookings open, and registration details are shared with buyers at the time of inquiry.

Can I visit a completed project before booking?+

Absolutely — we regularly arrange visits to completed communities like Maruthi Enclave and Maruthi Nilayam so you can see build quality firsthand.

What areas of Hyderabad do you currently build in?+

Our project experience is concentrated in Kukatpally, Miyapur, Nizampet, Kompally, Tellapur and Bachupally, with plot-based construction considered on a case-by-case basis elsewhere.
